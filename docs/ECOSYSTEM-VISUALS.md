The initial interpretation emphasized textured planetary bodies, but operator review corrected that: the approved concept requires dark volumetric orbs, directional light/shadow, atmospheric rims, a strong gold Olympus centerpiece, and layered stars/constellations. A functional layout with gradient discs did not meet that standard.

The revised scene uses `src/assets/ecosystem-planet-gold.png`, generated with the built-in image generator, composited with hue treatments for project bodies. Planet area is a bounded display of recorded vault task counts; unknown stays explicitly unmeasured. These are shared terrain textures with different colors, not unique simulated planets or a physical orbital simulation.

Directional sunlight from upper left, pale atmospheric rim, deep lower-right shadow. Entire sphere visible, centered, no stars, text, symbols, rings or backdrop. Premium Olympus central celestial body and recolorable UI asset; detailed surface remains legible at 180px.

## Orb correction after operator review
The literal rocky-planet treatment was rejected. The current scene uses layered volumetric shading with a faint blurred cloud contribution, dark hemispheres, colored atmospheric rims and distinct colors for up to eight visible projects. The surface asset is now subordinate rather than the visual subject. Connection endpoints anchor to the sphere center, independently of label height. Clean cubic paths carry staggered animated light; this is a decorative information-link metaphor, not live network telemetry. Manual pause, hidden-document pause and reduced motion apply. Two successive native captures were inspected against the reference; the second softened specular highlights and removed the glass-like interior arc. Test coverage measures all eight endpoints and verifies light movement, pause/resume, and reduced motion.

## Composition correction
The operator rejected the preceding captures: functional connection tests did not establish reference fidelity. This pass replaces the equal radial layout with asymmetric, count-aware positions, places labels beside the orbs on wide panels, shortens the native eight-project scene from 668px to approximately 464px overall, and masks the environment away from the central space. Small panels retain below-orb labels. Eight real projects remain visible; equal recorded task counts retain equal sphere sizes. Central hierarchy comes from a larger gold hub, without inventing task values.

Native pass one exposed excessive height and broad lighting. Pass two reduced scene height and narrowed the upper-left highlights, retaining dark interiors and fine center-anchored relay paths. Browser review also exposed empty lower space for four-project fixtures; their positions now fill upper/lower corners. This is a composition improvement, not a claim of pixel-identical reference rendering: the current mountain asset and smoother orb material still differ.
